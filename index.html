Rollcall reads project structure, not media. Speech is transcribed on-device, and only the text of your command and the timeline metadata it needs are sent to the model.

  • Every action is a normal undo step
  • Push-to-talk by default; nothing records in the background

    How it works

    Talk at the speed you think. Your editor keeps up.

    Say it how you’d say it

    Ramble, pause, correct yourself mid-sentence. Rollcall hears timestamps, clip names and rough directions, and understands what you meant.

    It becomes an edit plan

    Fillers and repeats go. What’s left is a short list of concrete edits at real timecodes, checked against your open project before anything moves.

    Applied inside your editor

    Each edit runs through the editor’s own scripting layer, so it lands as a normal, undoable change in DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro or CapCut.
