# 005 · Find a shot by describing it

**Status:** Idea · **Updated:** 2026-09-25

**In one line:** "the shot where she laughs by the car" jumps straight to it.

## Problem
Scrubbing through hours of footage to find one moment.

## How it works
1. Rollcall indexes clips on the Mac: transcript, faces, objects, places, mood.
2. Describe the shot out loud.
3. Matching clips appear with timecodes; say "use that one" to drop it on the timeline.

## First version
- Search by transcript and simple scene tags.
- Search runs fully on the Mac.
