# 010 · Hook and retention coach

**Status:** Idea · **Updated:** 2026-09-25

**In one line:** for shorts, Rollcall flags where viewers are likely to drop off and offers stronger openings.

## Problem
Most shorts lose viewers in the first seconds, and creators can't tell why.

## How it works
1. Rollcall reviews the short: first seconds, pacing, dead air, caption timing.
2. It marks likely drop-off points.
3. It offers 3 alternative hooks (a different first shot, caption or cut) to try by voice.
