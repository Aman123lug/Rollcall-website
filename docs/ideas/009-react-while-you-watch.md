# 009 · React while you watch

**Status:** Idea · **Updated:** 2026-09-25

**In one line:** play the cut and just react ("boring", "love this"), and Rollcall tightens the edit around it.

## Problem
Knowing a part drags is easy. Working out exactly what to cut is not.

## How it works
1. Play the cut and react out loud as you watch.
2. Reactions are pinned to timecodes ("boring", "too long", "love this").
3. Rollcall proposes changes: trim the slow parts, protect the ones you liked.
4. Apply all, or review one by one.

## First version
- Three reactions: cut, tighten, keep.
- Proposal before any change.

## Open questions
- How aggressive should trims be by default?
- Could it learn what "boring" means to you?
