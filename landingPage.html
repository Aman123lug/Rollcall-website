Three steps between your idea and the cut.

You keep your editor, your project files and your shortcuts. Rollcall sits alongside and takes the tedious part.

1

Speak

Hold a key and describe the change the way you'd tell a colleague. Timestamps, colours, clip names and rough directions all work.

“At fourteen seconds, add a caption that says welcome back, yellow, bold, bottom centre.”
2

Understand

The agent reads your open project: timeline, clip names, markers and the current playhead. It resolves “this clip” and “here” to real positions.

Resolved: t=14.0s · track V2 · style: bold · color #F5B841 · anchor bottom-center
3

Act inside the editor

Each step becomes a tool call into the editor's own scripting layer, so the result is a normal, undoable edit in your project file.

addTextLayer({ track: "V2", start: 14.0, text: "Welcome back", … })

Things you can just say.

No fixed phrases to memorise. These are real examples from testing.

Cutting and timing

  • Cut here and delete the pause before it.
  • Trim the last two seconds off this clip.
  • Move the B-roll to start at 0:38.
  • Speed up everything between 1:10 and 1:30 by 1.5x.

Text and captions

  • Add a lower third with my name, white on dark.
  • Auto-caption this section in the yellow style I used earlier.
  • Make that title bigger and move it up a bit.
  • Change every caption to the brand font.

Colour and look

  • Warm this shot up slightly.
  • Match the colour of this clip to the previous one.
  • Apply the LUT from the intro to the whole V1 track.
  • Add a soft vignette from 0:20 to 0:45.

Audio

  • Duck the music under the voiceover.
  • Mute the music from 0:14 until the next cut.
  • Remove background noise on A1.
  • Fade the last clip out over two seconds.

Your footage stays on your Mac.

Rollcall reads project structure, not media. Speech is transcribed on-device, and only the text of your command and the timeline metadata it needs are sent to the model.

  • No video or audio files uploaded
  • Every action is a normal undo step
  • Push-to-talk by default; nothing records in the background
